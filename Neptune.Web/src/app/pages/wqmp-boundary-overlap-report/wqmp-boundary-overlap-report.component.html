Each row is a pair of Water Quality Management Plan boundaries that cover the same ground. Only Active plans that use the Simplified modeling approach are compared, and only overlaps larger than 0.05 acres are listed.

A plan treats runoff when it has at least one simple BMP of a modeled type with % of Site Treated and Wet Weather % Captured filled in. Where only one plan of a pair treats runoff, the model gives the shared ground to that plan. Where both do, or neither does, the model counts the shared ground under both plans until one of the boundaries is corrected.
